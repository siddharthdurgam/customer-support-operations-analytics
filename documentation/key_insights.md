# Key Insights & Business Recommendations

## Executive Findings

### Support Channel Demand

Email is the largest support channel with approximately 8.4K tickets, followed by Mobile App with approximately 8.1K.

**Recommendation:** Prioritize staffing, routing, and self-service improvements for the highest-volume channels.

### Issue Category Demand

Refund Request is the largest issue category at approximately 7.0K tickets, followed by Technical Issue at approximately 6.9K.

**Recommendation:** Investigate recurring root causes instead of treating high-volume issues only at the ticket level.

### Resolution Complexity

Damaged Product cases have the highest average resolution time among the issue categories analyzed.

**Recommendation:** Review escalation paths, evidence requirements, and cross-team dependencies for damaged-product cases.

### Backlog Pressure

Open tickets represent the largest ticket-status group at approximately 10.5K tickets, or 26.32% of total tickets.

**Recommendation:** Introduce backlog-aging reviews, priority-based queues, and escalation rules for long-running open tickets.

### Regional Distribution

Central and North show the highest ticket volumes at roughly 9K tickets each, while East is materially lower at roughly 4.5K.

**Recommendation:** Use regional demand patterns when planning staffing and operational capacity, while avoiding unnecessary regional differentiation where satisfaction remains similar.

### Agent Workload

Ticket volumes are relatively balanced across the four support agents, with broadly similar satisfaction scores.

**Recommendation:** Maintain balanced allocation while monitoring resolution time and satisfaction together rather than optimizing for volume alone.

## Recommended Management Priorities

1. Reduce the open-ticket backlog.
2. Improve workflows for complex issue categories.
3. Focus capacity planning on high-volume support channels.
4. Investigate recurring refund and technical issues.
5. Monitor customer satisfaction alongside operational efficiency KPIs.
