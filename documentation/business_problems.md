# Business Problem & Analytical Scope

## Business Problem

Customer support operations generate large volumes of tickets across multiple channels, issue categories, regions, and agents. Management needs a consolidated view of demand, backlog, customer satisfaction, and resolution efficiency to identify operational priorities.

## Analytical Goal

Build an end-to-end analytics solution that transforms raw support-ticket data into decision-ready KPIs and recommendations.

## Key Business Questions

1. What is the total support workload?
2. Which channels generate the highest ticket demand?
3. Which issue categories create the most complaints?
4. Where is support demand concentrated geographically?
5. How large is the open-ticket backlog?
6. How balanced is workload across support agents?
7. How does customer satisfaction vary across operational dimensions?
8. How does ticket volume change over time?
9. Is resolution efficiency improving or declining?
10. Which operational areas should management prioritize?

## Deliverables

- Cleaned and validated analytical dataset
- SQL data-quality and business-analysis queries
- Python operational analysis
- DAX KPI measures
- Power BI executive dashboard
- Business insights and recommendations
